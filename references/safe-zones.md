# Safe zones and restricted topics

Used by `hook-marketer` (section `Безпечні зони`) and `review-council` (role SMM-фахівець). Canvas for every number below: **1080×1920 px** (9:16). Margins are measured from the matching edge of the frame inward.

Every row carries a source URL, the check date and a mark: «офіційне» (a number or sentence printed by the platform itself, or a number computed only by arithmetic from a printed percentage) or «неофіційне» (a third-party measurement or a community value). Check date for every source on this page: **2026-11-16** (the pages were opened that day).

**Re-check rule.** Platform interfaces change. Before relying on this file, re-open the official links below and compare; if a date here is older than 90 days, or the app visibly changed, re-run the research (open the official pages, download the official safe-zone template where one is named, measure it) and update the dates. Never replace a number without a source.

**Scope warning.** No platform publishes safe-zone numbers for ORGANIC posts (creator uploads). The only official numbers are for ADS (Instagram Reels ads, TikTok In-Feed ads, YouTube/Google video ads). Organic posts have extra interface (longer captions, hashtags, sound label, comments), so treat the ad margins as a minimum.

## Subtitle band

No platform names a "subtitle zone". The band below is derived by us, from the margins above. Mark: неофіційне (derived).

- Cross-platform safe rectangle (strictest value of each side): top 288, bottom 672, left 120, right 192 px, so x 120 to 888 and y 288 to 1248 on the canvas.
- Subtitle band: text boxes fully inside y 960 to 1248 (that is 672 to 960 px from the bottom edge), horizontally inside x 120 to 888. Keep the lowest text line's bottom edge at least 672 px from the bottom.
- Hook text in the first seconds: inside y 288 to 960 (the upper part of the safe rectangle, above the subtitle band).
- If the video is published to ONE platform only, the margins of that platform's row may be used instead, and the band moves accordingly. The report must say which rule was applied.

How to use this section: compare the video's spoken words, on-screen text, claims and products with the categories above and name the category and the page in the `Ризики обмежень` section. A topic that appears on a page is a risk to check, not proof that the video breaks the rule.
